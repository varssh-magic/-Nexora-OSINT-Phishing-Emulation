# PHISHING SIMULATION REPORT

## 1. Objective

To evaluate how OSINT-derived organizational context
can be used to construct a convincing spear-phishing
pretext.

## 2. Target Organization

Nexora Technologies

Domain: nexora.test

## 3. Target Persona

Priya Sharma

Department: HR

Email: priya.sharma@nexora.test

## 4. Attack Scenario

HR employee benefits portal update.

## 5. Email Characteristics

Subject: Important: Employee Benefits Portal Update

Format: HTML email

Link: Local training portal

## 6. Header Analysis

Analyzed:
From
Reply-To
Return-Path
Authentication-Results

## 7. Authentication Assessment

SPF and DMARC policies were inspected using
the controlled local DNS environment.

## 8. MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

T1589
T1598
T1566.002

## 9. Outcome

A controlled spear-phishing email simulation
was generated and analyzed.

No real employees were contacted.
No credentials were collected.
No external email delivery was performed.
